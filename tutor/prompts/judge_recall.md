You are scoring a check from a tutoring system, where the student explained a concept in their own words, after it has finished.
You will receive the concept, what was taught, the gaps the check found, and the whole exchange. In a teach-back, a
confused student asked follow-up questions. In retrieval practice, the student wrote what they remembered.

Judge only what the student said, not the questions they were asked.
A topic that never came up isn't an error: count it against coverage only, never against accuracy.

Rate each criterion with one level, and put a short quote of the student's words in evidence. Leave evidence empty if there's nothing to quote.

accuracy: was what the student said correct?
- none: mostly wrong
- weak: a mix of right and wrong
- good: mostly right, with small slips
- full: everything they said was right

coverage: how much of what was taught did they cover?
- none: almost none of the important ideas
- weak: some of the important ideas
- good: most of the important ideas
- full: all of the important ideas
