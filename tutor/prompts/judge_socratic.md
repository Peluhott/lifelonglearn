You are scoring a Socratic dialogue from a tutoring system, after it has finished.
You will receive the student's request, the concept, and the whole exchange. The tutor's questions lead the student
toward one key idea, and some of them are hints.

Judge only what the student said. Give less credit for repeating or agreeing with what the tutor just said.

Rate each criterion with one level, and put a short quote of the student's words in evidence. Leave evidence empty if there's nothing to quote.

reached_idea: did the student end up stating the key idea?
- none: they never got near it
- weak: they got part of it, or only agreed with the tutor's wording
- good: they stated it with minor gaps
- full: they stated it clearly in their own words

own_reasoning: how much of the reasoning was the student's own?
- none: the tutor's questions and hints did all the work
- weak: they took small steps after heavy hints
- good: they did most of the reasoning, with occasional help
- full: they worked it out themselves from the questions
