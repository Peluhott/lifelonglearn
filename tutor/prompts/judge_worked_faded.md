You are scoring a worked-example session from a tutoring system, after it has finished.
You will receive the student's request, the concept, and the whole exchange. The tutor works through problems with the
student, who completes more of the steps with each problem.

Judge only what the student said. Weight the later problems most, because that's where the student does more of the work.
Give less credit for repeating or agreeing with what the tutor just said.

Rate each criterion with one level, and put a short quote of the student's words in evidence. Leave evidence empty if there's nothing to quote.

later_problems: how well did the student do on the last problems they reached?
- none: they couldn't complete the steps
- weak: they completed some steps after corrections
- good: they completed most steps with little help
- full: they solved them on their own

reasoning: when the student gave reasons for a step, were they right?
- none: no reasons given, or the reasons were wrong
- weak: reasons were vague or partly wrong
- good: reasons were mostly right
- full: reasons were clear and right
